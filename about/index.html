About Hero Laboratory

Hero Laboratory publishes practical, beginner-friendly guides, ebooks and ChatGPT prompt packs for people who want to earn extra income online with free tools – side hustles, freelancing, Threads and small business marketing.

What we believe

  • Honest numbers. We never promise income or followers. Results depend on effort, offer and market.
  • Free tools first. Everything we teach works with free ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Canva and CapCut.
  • One clear next step. Every guide ends with something you can do today.

Editorial policy & AI use

Our guides and ebooks are researched and outlined by our team, drafted with the help of AI writing tools, then fact-checked, edited and organized by hand. We update guides when tools or platforms change and show the "Updated" date on every guide.

Some guides recommend our own ebooks. Product links go to our Gumroad store. We do not accept paid placements.
